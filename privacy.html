Demo privacy notice

How we handle your details when you try the Aerset demo or register interest. The demo runs on simulated data and is for trying the product only.

Who we are

This notice is from Aerset (not yet incorporated), run by Hardik Gumber, who is responsible for your data. Contact: aerset.hq@gmail.com.

The quickest option: no account at all

You don't need to give us anything to try the demo. Open the operator dashboard and choose Try with demo data to explore a ready-made organisation. Everything in it is simulated.

What we collect if you create a demo account, and why

Customer appEmail address, password, mobile number, and your choices about delivery texts and offers.
Operator dashboardOrganisation name and type, postcode (optional), work email and password.
While you use the demoWhat you enter, such as jobs, notes and support messages. If you search for an address or postcode, our server sends just that search to postcodes.io or OpenStreetMap Nominatim to look it up; your IP address isn't passed on.
Your location (optional)The customer app can use your browser's location, but only if you allow it, and only for this demo.
Security logYour account ID, role, what you did and when. For some security events, such as failed sign-ins or a failed handover check, it also records your IP address. Your sign-up consent choices are logged too.

We use these details only to give you access to the demo and to keep it secure. We don't send marketing, and we don't sell your details. Texts and emails in the demo, including sign-in codes and password-reset links, are simulated and shown on screen; nothing is actually sent to your phone or inbox.

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in letting people try the demo and keeping it secure. Where you choose to share your location or opt in to something, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Please don't use the demo for real patient, sample or health information. It isn't built or approved for real data.

Where your data is held

The demo runs on Render, which hosts it for us. The demo's web service and database are both in Frankfurt, Germany, so demo data is held in the EU and isn't transferred outside the UK or EEA for hosting.

Other services your browser connects to

Maps in the demo load from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), and the staff dashboard loads its map software (Leaflet) from unpkg.com. Like any website, these services see your IP address when your browser requests them. The demo uses no analytics and no tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

The demo runs on a free hosting plan with no backups, and its database expires about 30 days after it's created. Demo accounts, data and the security log may be wiped at any time, and at the latest when the database is reset. You can ask us to delete your data sooner by emailing aerset.hq@gmail.com.

Register-interest form

If you fill in our register-interest form, your answers are processed by Formspree (a US-based form service) and delivered to us by email. We keep them for no longer than 24 months. See the form's privacy note for details.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive a copy to take elsewhere. Email aerset.hq@gmail.com and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

Cookies and sign-in

This website (aerset.github.io) and the demo set no cookies. To keep you signed in, the demo stores a short-lived sign-in token in your browser. It lasts 15 minutes and can be renewed for up to 7 days. On the staff dashboard it's kept in the open tab and cleared when you close it. In the customer app it's kept in your browser's local storage until you sign out.
